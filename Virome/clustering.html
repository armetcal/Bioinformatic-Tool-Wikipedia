Viromics Step 4: Clustering Viral Contigs

Overview

This uses MMSeqs to cluster contigs by genetic similarity. This is analogous to creating OTUs or ASVs during 16S sequencing.

Clustering is important for statistics. Individual contigs are usually too rare to run reliable statistics, and tiny differences in contig sequences are unlikely to be biologically meaningful. Therefore, clustering gives you the best of both worlds: fewer false positives (by reducing the sheer number of contigs) and fewer false negatives (since clustering gives you a better abundance estimate for any meaningfully distinct group of contigs, improving statistical power).
